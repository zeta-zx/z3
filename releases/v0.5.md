## What's new

### Music Discovery & Recommendations
- **Personalized recommendations on Home.** Discover songs based on your listening taste, including familiar favorites and tracks you may not have heard yet.
- **Genre and playlist discovery.** Browse genre-focused collections and popular playlists, including worldwide and rock selections.
- **Improved radio and autoplay.** Get more varied recommendations, with fewer duplicate songs and artists, and smoother continuation when a radio queue runs low.

### Search
- **Richer search suggestions.** See relevant songs, artists, albums, playlists, and music videos while searching.
- **More complete search results.** Album and playlist results are available alongside songs, with YouTube Music category handling improved for current and legacy result formats.
- **Music video search.** Find videos related to the song you are playing, with the closest match selected automatically and other matches available as alternatives.

### Player Improvements
- **Video mode.** Watch a music video for the current song in the player. Video playback pauses Zeta audio to prevent both from playing at once. YouTube captions can be enabled when available.
- **More stable player blur.** The player capsule blur no longer rebuilds its glass effect during hover resizing.
- **Prefer downloaded matches.** Search playback can use a matching downloaded track from your library when available.
- **Improved playback transitions.** Track changes pause reliably before the next song starts.

### Accessibility & Release
- **Keyboard-friendly playback controls.** Track and queue playback controls use accessible buttons, and dialogs can be dismissed with Escape.
- **Version 0.5.0.** Android version code is 5.
