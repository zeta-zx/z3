## What's new in v0.6.0

### Connect with Wayship & P2P Cloud Mesh
- **One-click Connect with Wayship (`Settings → Nearby & Wayship`)**: Sign in with Wayship on desktop (`127.0.0.1` loopback consent) or Android (`wayship.link/connect` or `wsa_…` app token) with zero developer setup.
- **Automatic cross-network P2P sync**: Zeta devices signed into the same Wayship account automatically discover each other via `/api/peers/announce` and `PeerEvent` over `wss://wayship.link/api/_ephaptic`, negotiate a direct WebRTC `RTCDataChannel` using Wayship's STUN servers, and sync listening stats, playlists, and favourites in the background—plus one-click **Sync & Remote**, **Play Together**, and **Send Backup**.

### Custom Music Video Player & Seamless Playback
- **Custom HTML5 Video Player**: Replaced the embedded YouTube `<iframe>` in the **Video** tab with a custom native `<video>` player (`MusicVideoPlayer.svelte`) streaming up to 1080p VP9/MP4 directly.
- **Automatic black-bar removal**: Samples video frames onto an offscreen canvas to detect symmetric letterbox and pillarbox bars (both full-range and studio-range MPEG black) and crops them away so the video fills the stage cleanly.
- **Live video-frame ambient lighting**: Paints the cropped video frame into a blurred, saturated ambient backdrop canvas and extracts 3 regional frame colours (`--vid-1..3` / `--art-1..3`) so surrounding space reacts live to the video's colours.
- **Seamless song ↔ video continuation**: When the selected video shares the same YouTube ID as the playing song, Zeta's main `<audio>` engine continues uninterrupted with zero gap while the video locks to `playerState.currentTime`. When switching to an alternate video recording, playback starts at the song's current timestamp and seeks the main audio player back on exit.

### YouTube Playback, Search & Browser Cookie Import
- **Fixed YouTube `HTTP 403` streaming & download errors**: Switched ranged `googlevideo.com` requests on desktop (`electron/routes/music.ts`) and Android (`ZetaMediaDownloadPlugin.java`) from `&range=` query parameters to standard HTTP `Range: bytes=…` headers, with automatic BotGuard `PoToken` + `Innertube` session refresh retry before falling back to `yt-dlp`.
- **Improved YouTube Music search ranking**: Combines `{ type: 'song' }` (with and without `" by "`) and `{ type: 'video' }` shelves in parallel so official tracks that only exist under YouTube's Videos shelf (e.g. *"King"* by *Years & Years*) rank at `#1`, while keeping karaoke, tribute, and cover versions further down in the results list.
- **One-click browser cookie import (`Settings → YouTube`)**: Scans all installed Chromium-family (`Chrome`, `Brave`, `Chromium`, `Edge`, `Vivaldi`, `Opera`, `Helium`) and `Firefox` profiles, checks their SQLite cookie stores for active YouTube logins, displays the signed-in account email as eyebrow text with a one-click **Import cookies** button, shows non-logged-in browser profiles grayed out (`Cookies found · YouTube not logged in`), and retains the separate manual Netscape `cookies.txt` import.

### Backups, Muzza Interop, Nearby Pairing & Polish
- **512 MiB `.zeta` & Muzza backup limit**: Raised the backup size limit from `128 MiB` to `512 MiB` across desktop, Android, and peer transfers.
- **Muzza embedded cover art & full stats carry-over**: Preserves embedded song cover art (`data:image/…;base64,…` and local audio tag extraction via `localPath`), playlist covers (`playlist.thumbnailUrl`), and full listening history (`event` rows plus any aggregate `song.totalPlayTime` balance) while merging into Zeta's existing stats instead of wiping them.
- **Tailscale + One-click "Pair on both devices"**: Added Tailscale CGNAT (`100.64.0.0/10`) support and UDP broadcast/Tailnet discovery on port `42534` so tapping a pairing mode on both devices pairs them automatically without scanning a QR code, and automatically syncs listening stats, favourites, and playlists on connect.
- **Android widget Material You option**: Added a **Home-screen widget theme** setting (**Track artwork** palette extraction vs Android 12+ **Material You** dynamic system colors) alongside track thumbnail display on the widget.
- **Stage view living colour & single-track preloader**: Enabled full-intensity `<AmbientBackground>` living colour in **Stage** view to match **Lyrics** view, and tuned the upcoming track preloader to preload **1** upcoming track instead of 2.
